# Privacy And Storage

This page is kept for older links. The current storage and privacy contract is
documented in [storage.md](storage.md).

Exact MCP server/tool names are opaque private local data. Their activity
storage, display-safety, and not-share-safe output rules are summarized in
[mcp-tool-call-attribution.md](mcp-tool-call-attribution.md).

Captured provider call IDs, arguments, status/timing, results, and literal facts
are also private local content. Their content-policy, limits, and retrieval rules are in
[mcp-exchange-capture.md](mcp-exchange-capture.md).
